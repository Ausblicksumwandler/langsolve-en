\part[Practice Exercises]{The \textsanskrit{ब्राह्मण} is always hungry} \label{part:enprac}
Do you really want to do this?
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Which features (subsections) of the basic structure does a complete perspective absolutely require? Derive the simplest complete language structure.
    \item Can general complex numbers be expressed using the methods in \texttt{Numbers}? How would such expressions look, and what methods must be added to give completeness?
    \item Any perspective needs its corresponding $\vm{T}$. What changes are observed in the verb form calculation algorithm (\autoref{fig:enverbZ2}) if the rows and columns of $\vm{T}$ are shuffled around from its standard configuration? What must be changed along with this shuffling to ensure that the final verb form remains the same for any given transformation?
    \item Is it possible to achieve a 3-dimensional $\vm{T}$? What would such a situation entail?
\end{enumerate}
